Theory and procedure

Aim: to determine the support reactions of simple and compound beams and verify them against the equations of equilibrium.

Equilibrium

The beam is treated as a rigid body: it does not bend, and stress and failure are not considered. A beam at rest satisfies ΣFy = 0 and ΣM = 0 about any point.

Simple beam (pin at A, balance at B, length L): RB = ΣWx / L and RA = ΣW − RB.

Compound beam: pin at A (x = 0), roller and balance at B (x = 0.4 m), internal hinge at 0.6 m and roller at C (x = 1.0 m). The hinge carries no moment, so the right segment is solved first and its hinge force is applied to the left segment.

Procedure

  1. Open the simulation and note the initial reactions at A and B (beam self-weight only).
  2. Enter a weight W and its distance x, then add it.
  3. The Python backend returns the reactions RA and RB, which are recorded in the observation table.
  4. Check that ΣFy = 0 and ΣM = 0 in the conclusion.
  5. Download the report as a PDF.

Assumed values

  • L = 1 m, self-weight 4 N
  • Rigid beam (no deflection, no failure)
  • Wmax = 50 N per added weight
