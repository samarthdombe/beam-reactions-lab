Determination of support reactions of simple and compound beams

Load a rigid beam, read the support reactions, and check them against the equations of equilibrium.

What you will do

  1. Choose a simple or compound beam.
  2. Add weights at chosen distances from the left support.
  3. Read the reactions at supports A and B.
  4. Check that ΣF = 0 and ΣM = 0 hold for the rigid beam.
