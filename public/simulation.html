Simulation: simple beam

The beam is treated as rigid: it does not bend or break, so only the support reactions change. Drag the blue handle along the beam to choose where the next weight goes. On the compound beam you can also drag the middle support (roller B); moving it restarts the experiment.

Conclusion

Theory: A rigid body is in static equilibrium when ΣF = 0 and ΣM = 0. The reactions include the beam's self-weight and every weight added so far; on the compound beam the roller at C also carries part of the load.
