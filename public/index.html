Determination of support reactions of simple and compound beams

Load a timber beam, read the balance, and check the result against the moment law of equilibrium.

What you will do

  1. Choose a simple or compound beam.
  2. Add weights at chosen distances from the left support.
  3. Compare the balance reading with the calculated reaction.
  4. Keep loading to see the beam bend, crack and fail.
